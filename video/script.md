## Optional voiceover (72 words, about 25 seconds at a calm pace)

> A lunar outpost runs on stored reserves, and every decision spends one of them. In Outpost Zero
> students keep four crew alive for five cycles. Here, the last cycle: a grow cycle lifts the food
> store, and takes power and water to do it. The report shows every change, and why it happened,
> with NASA sources attached. Trade offs, made visible, one decision at a time.

Timing notes:

- Start the voice at 0.4 s so it does not collide with the fade in.
- Land "food store" near 11.4 s when the report appears.
- Pause at 13.4 s; the table is the proof and does not need talking over.
- Finish before 22 s and leave the end card silent.

Record a safety take, keep the microphone level steady, and export the audio as AAC or leave the
master WAV beside the video. Do not add music unless the licence is clear and it sits well below
the voice.
