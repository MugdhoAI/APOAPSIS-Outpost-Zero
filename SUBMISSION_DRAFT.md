## One sentence summary

Outpost Zero is a short interactive lunar outpost game that helps students experience how decisions about power, water, oxygen, food production, and radiation protection affect one another.

## Project description — draft

Outpost Zero puts players in charge of a fictional lunar south polar outpost for five operational cycles. Each turn, players choose how to maintain or improve a critical system, then see the immediate costs, resource changes, and mission consequences. A solar array upgrade can improve later power generation, a regolith shelter can improve a simplified radiation protection index, and greenhouse production can increase food reserves while consuming power and water. A mission debrief explains the trade offs and links them to NASA science resources.

The game is designed as an educational model, not a real mission planning or crew safety tool. All resource points, cycle lengths, thresholds, and outcomes are simplified gameplay assumptions. The project uses NASA's LOLA lunar polar illumination product to explain why solar access varies around the lunar poles, alongside NASA research on radiation, life support, lunar power, and space crops.
